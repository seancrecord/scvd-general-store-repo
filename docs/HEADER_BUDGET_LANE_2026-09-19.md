# The header guard learns the lane — September 19, 2026

The same blind spot as [the OpenAPI document's](OPENAPI_READ_BUDGET_2026-09-19.md),
found the same night by reading the live store instead of the test
fixture. A stock Node client refuses any response whose header block
passes 16,384 bytes, and this store's widest 402 is the one place that
line is near: fifteen signed x402 offers (five rails, three tiers) and,
since the [native tips release](MPP_NATIVE_TIPS_2026-09-19.md), three
Payment challenges in `WWW-Authenticate` beside them. Read live at
03:49 UTC, the widest door (`/api/buy/graffiti_on_a_train`) carried
14,017 bytes: 1,343 under the 15,360-byte line the guards hold, 2,367
under the cliff.

The guards could not see it. `test/five-network-header-budget.spec.ts`
(the September 6 rails release) walks every door under five rails and
holds the line, but its bindings never enabled the native lane: the
widest door measured 11,494 there, the three challenges (2,128 bytes)
absent. `test/challenge-header-budget.spec.ts` projects the widest shelf
from a per-offer envelope, and its typed "nine offers, three rails" had
stood for thirteen days after the fourth and fifth rails opened; the
lane's challenges were not in its arithmetic at all.

## What changed

- **The five-rail guard builds the lane.** `MPP_CHECKOUT_ENABLED` and a
  fixture challenge key ride its bindings, so every door it measures
  carries the challenge list a live door carries; it asserts one
  challenge per tier on each door (derived from `priceTiersUsdc`, the
  publication pages from their accepts), and names the widest door and
  its bytes when the line is crossed. Measured after: the widest door at
  13,574 built here.
- **The edge's lines are counted.** Cloudflare adds nine header lines no
  local build writes (`report-to`, `nel`, `date`, `cf-ray`, `alt-svc`,
  `transfer-encoding`, `x-robots-tag`, `server`, `connection`), 443 bytes
  on the widest door that day: 14,017 live against 13,574 here on the
  same offers and challenges. `EDGE_HEADER_BYTES = 450` is added to every
  local total, measured and dated, so the guard's number is the client's.
- **The envelope guard derives its widest shelf** from `checkoutNetworks`
  under every rail times `PWID_TIER_MULTIPLIERS`, fifteen today, and
  budgets one Payment challenge (`CHALLENGE_ENVELOPE_BUDGET = 800`;
  measured 709) the way it budgets one signed offer, on the widest door
  with the lane on.

Nothing in the store's own bytes changed: the offers, the challenges and
the headers are as they were. The guards now measure them.

## What would catch it going stale

- `test/five-network-header-budget.spec.ts`: every menu and publication
  quote under five rails and the lane, edge lines counted, under 15,360.
- `test/challenge-header-budget.spec.ts`: one offer under 650 bytes, one
  challenge under 800, the widest shelf derived.
- `npm run doors:check` reads the live doors; the live number above is
  the one to re-take when a rail, a tier or a header line is added.

## The room that is left

1,343 bytes live. A sixth rail costs three offers on a tipping door,
about 2,200 bytes: over the line, under the cliff by a hair. The
[rails record](../PAYMENT_RAILS.md) names no sixth rail, and the
[five-rail guard](../test/five-network-header-budget.spec.ts) already
moves the signed-offer header mirror into the body on quotes that would
overflow; if the day comes, that mechanism is the lever, not the line.
