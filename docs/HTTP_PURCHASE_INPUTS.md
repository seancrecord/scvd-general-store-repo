# HTTP purchase inputs, the probe rule, and free price discovery

`GET /api/buy/{item_id}` answers a bare request with `402 Payment Required` whether or not the item takes input. This is the probe rule, first set on 2026-07-26 when Bazaar and x402scout both missed every door that refused before quoting, and restored on 2026-09-11 after seven hours without it. The 402 carries the signable terms in `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` and, in the body, `required_params`, `required_params_note` and `input_contract_url` naming what the door needs and where its full contract is. The Bazaar discovery extension repeats the same requirement under `extensions.bazaar` with a `retry_url_template`. A stock x402 client, indexer or directory checker that probes bare and expects a 402 therefore sees every door as alive.

A request that is not a probe is validated before any terms go out. That is every signed request, and every unsigned request that supplies all of the item's required inputs. Missing, empty, whitespace-only or malformed inputs on such a request receive the field refusal, with `charged:false`, `input_field` and `input_contract_url`, and no `PAYMENT-REQUIRED`, `X-PAYMENT-REQUIRED` or `accepts` offer. This keeps the half of BUY-002 that protects a buyer: a client that composed a purchase learns of garbage before it signs, and a signed request with garbage is refused before the gate, no money moved.

The distinction is one predicate, `isProbe` in `src/routes/door-checks.ts`: unsigned, and lacking at least one required input. An unsigned probe still passes the encoding and type safety checks that never depended on the probe rule.

For price-only discovery without a request to the door, read `/api/catalog/v1` or `/menu/{item_id}?view=compact`, both free. The compact item contract includes `price_usdc`, offered price tiers and `input_schema`. Its `checkout` contract declares `bare_probe_answers_402:true` and `valid_inputs_required_before_quote:false`. Catalog prices are discovery data, not a signed authorization to spend; the fresh quote remains the payment authority.

Both the main store and the smaller doors Worker apply the same rules. Requests requiring the store's private field-wallet capability reach the store through the existing service binding. Signed requests still reach the store directly. An authenticated retained purchase remains eligible for original-goods recovery; stricter rules for new purchases never authorize another charge to retrieve old goods.

What was tried and withdrawn: PR #636 made every unsigned request validate its inputs before terms, so 25 of 32 doors answered a bare probe with 400. x402-list's checker counted 7 alive within fifteen minutes and marked the store degraded, and the store's own free preflight would have failed the same doors on its `status-402` check. The corrections ledger carries the entry.
