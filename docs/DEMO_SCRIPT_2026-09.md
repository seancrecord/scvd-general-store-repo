# The 60-second demo — script and shot list (roadmap S5)

Drafted 2026-09-02 for the keeper to record. The set is `/try`; nothing
here is rebuilt for the camera. Every line is a suggestion; the voice
is his (rule 7). Prices below are read off the shelf on the day of
recording — say what the page says, not what this file says.

## The shape

Four beats, fifteen seconds each. One browser window, one wallet, no
slides. Show the page before saying what it does.

| beat | on screen | say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · before | `/api/preflight/v1` doc, then paste any x402 URL into the form on `/conformance` (or curl it) | "Before an agent pays a door it has never used, this checks that the door can be paid. Free, no account. It names every check and it names what one probe cannot see." |
| 2 · after | `/conformance`, paste a signed offer or receipt (any issuer's) | "After it pays, this checks the signed receipt — ours or anyone else's. Same battery the weekly census runs, MIT-licensed, so a verdict that matters you reproduce offline." |
| 3 · the till | `/try`, press the button, sign once in the wallet | "The cheapest real x402 purchase on the internet. One signature, no gas. The goods land here with a signed certificate." |
| 4 · verify | click the certificate's verify link | "Anyone can check it, free, forever, without asking us. Signed, dated, expires, and it says what it does not prove. Not escrow, not a rating, not a guarantee." |

Close on the storefront's first screen so the sixty words are the last
thing on camera.

## Transcript (as spoken, about 140 words)

> Every artifact is signed, expires, and names what we did not see.
>
> Not escrow. Not a rating. Not a guarantee. scvd.store.

## Shot notes

- Record at the browser's default zoom; the pages are set in a serif
  and read fine at 1080p.
- The wallet prompt is the only moment that is not ours; keep it in
  frame — it is the proof there is no gas and no install.
- If the free preflight finds something wrong with the door you paste,
  leave it in. The instrument finding something is the point.
- The transcript, posted beside the video, is the same words; put the
  storefront URL under it and nothing else.

## Where it goes

The keeper's press (rule 30): the video and transcript on the channels
he keeps. In the tree, only this script and, once recorded, one link
on `/try` — the counter is the demo; it is not rebuilt for one.
