### Batch 2 of N — rails, without hitting the books

The analysis already lives at
`docs/PROTOCOL_EXPANSION_2026-08.md`. Do not start a second
copy. This batch is the constraint: hands in every pot, and
none of it may buy us latency we do not want, a ledger that
lies, or regulation we do not want.

**Two lanes, do not mix.**

- **A — till-side.** Can an agent pay us? Additive,
  conservative, flag-dark. Unset flag = byte-identical
  store. This is how we earn the right to testify about a
  rail. Sales on a $0.001–$21 shelf will stay small.
- **B — instrument-side.** Can we read everyone else's
  protocol? Aggressive and cheap. A parser and a subject
  row. Does not touch the till, the 402 body, or the bank
  walk. This is the product.

Never let A's regulation get near B's independence.

**Circle Gateway is the worked example, not a build order
yet.** Latency is the wrong fear (off-chain credit is
faster). The real cost is the books: the bank walk reads
chains and Gateway lands in a batch minutes later. Shape
that already has a precedent (#23, penny sales):

1. A distinct ledger class with an explicit
   `pending_batch` state — not an absence the walk reads
   as a hole.
2. A reconciliation window the delivery audit respects,
   so a Gateway sale is not paged as possibly-undelivered
   money during the batch.
3. A stated product boundary: `settlement_attestation`
   sells *chain* confirmation. A Gateway payment cannot
   be attested that way until its batch lands. Say so on
   the item.
4. Cache the second facilitator's supported-kinds in KV
   (`SUPPORTED_KINDS_KV_KEY` is the CDP pattern) or a
   cold isolate reintroduces a round trip on the 402
   path — the `/api/buy/hello` ~1.14s lesson.

**Proposed rail run-through** (⚑ approve / edit — this is
the thing to run every new pot through):

1. Lane A or B? B cannot touch till, ledger, or 402 body.
2. Re-read primaries before any payment code. INDEX ONLY
   and [secondary] claims are not wire facts.
3. Unset flag = byte-identical store. Base stays
   `accepts[0]`. A second protocol's challenge header
   never displaces the x402 body.
4. Latency: new facilitator kinds cached in KV. A rail
   that misses its budget is withdrawn from the offer,
   never slows a healthy sale. Money fails closed;
   the offer is decoration.
5. Ledger: if settlement is not on-chain at mint, a
   distinct class with an explicit pending state. Bank
   walk and delivery audit get a window, not a hole.
   Own `railOf` bucket end to end or the books book it
   as Base (the Polygon bug, verbatim).
6. Certificate: `settlement_finality` on the artifact
   (`irreversible_onchain` /
   `accepted_offchain_pending_batch` /
   `reversible_window_open_until:<date>`). Product
   boundary stated on any SKU that sells chain
   confirmation.
7. Declines protocol-tagged from the first line.
   Unknown says unknown. One idempotency scope across
   protocols, delivery intent as the interlock.
8. Three regulation tests: do we hold anyone's money?
   Does anyone need an account? Is the money reversible
   after we signed something forever? Artifact-minting
   items never take a reversible rail.
9. Caps and sweeps on any receivable (Gateway undrawn
   balance, same shape as the Solana / Polygon $10
   caps). Number is keeper ink.
10. Copy: no protocol name until the flow runs.
    Draft + suggestion; he inks.
11. Dogfood: we buy something ourselves on the new
    rail and publish what happened, including failures.
