## 1. Saymon S. — payforapi.com (replying to 2026-08-28)

**Subject:** Re: the free preflight — your three questions, answered

Hello Saymon,

You wrote on 28 August and this is 4 September. That is a week, and the
delay is entirely ours — you asked the most concrete questions anyone on
this list has asked, and they sat. Sorry.

Your three, in order.

**1. What the check covers.**

One unauthenticated GET at the URL you name, read against 28 named defect
classes. Every check comes back by its name, pass or fail. There is no
score and no grade — just the list, so you can see which thing failed and
fix that thing. A sample of the named classes:

    no-402                    the door never asks for payment
    unparseable-challenge     it asks, but the challenge will not parse
    unsignable-offer          it parses, but no stock client can sign it
    wrong-network             the chain named is not the chain that receives
    amount-not-atomic         the amount is not in atomic units
    unpayable-payto           the receiving address cannot receive
    replay-accepted           the same payment authorisation works twice
    delivered-nothing         payment settled and no goods came back

**What it does not cover, plainly:** the free preflight does not spend
money. It checks that your 402 is shaped so a stock client *could* pay it.
It does not prove a settlement actually clears — that needs a real paid
walk, which is a separate, paid thing you do not need to start.

**2. A badge or a public link.**

Yes, though probably not the kind you are picturing, and the difference is
the point.

- The preflight itself returns JSON, immediately, free, no account.
- A dated public page — `scvd.store/passport/{your-host}` — is issued from
  our Sunday census once your door reads ready. It says who looked, when,
  and the date after which to stop trusting the reading.
- There is a chip for a README, markdown or HTML, nothing to claim and no
  permission to ask. It carries the observation date and goes **dark**
  rather than staying green if your door later leaves the ready side.

It never says "passed" or "certified", and we will not add those words. It
says you were observed, on a date, by someone outside — which is the only
part a counterparty can check for themselves.

**3. What to prepare.**

A URL. Nothing else. No account, no key, no wallet, nothing to install.

    curl -X POST https://scvd.store/api/preflight/v2 \
      -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      -d '{"url":"https://your-door/your-endpoint"}'

From a terminal: `npx scvd preflight <url>`
In CI: the same check as a GitHub Action, which fails the deploy on
`not_ready` and does not fail it on `unreachable`.

Reading is free, forever. If any of it is wrong about your door, tell us
and we will publish the correction against ourselves — that is what
`scvd.store/corrections` is for.

— the keeper, SCVD General Store (scvd.store)

## 2. GBLIN (replying to 2026-08-28)

Short, peer to peer, no pitch, nothing attached.

**Subject:** Re: — you are right, and we made the same mistake

Hello,

Thank you for the correction, and more for the part that was not one.

You are right to refuse "real agents" for payers you measured yourself and
found to be catalogue crawlers at about one percent. For what it is worth,
we publish no claim about your traffic — GBLIN appears in our raw preflight
field data and in no public verdict of ours — so there is nothing on our
side to retract. But the principle deserves an answer rather than silence.

We made precisely the mistake you are refusing to make. Earlier this year
the store had thousands of tool listings and no purchases, and we read it
as a funnel problem: agents arrive, see the price, leave. The honest answer
was that nobody could tell. Every MCP client announces itself in the
handshake and we were dropping that field on the floor, keeping a
User-Agent that most clients never meaningfully set. We were inferring our
own visitors while selling other people the discipline of observing theirs.
We capture the client name now and count crawlers apart from payers.

"That isn't pedantry — it is the only thing we have earned so far" is our
own house rule, put better than we have managed to put it.

Nothing attached, nothing to sign up for.

— the keeper, SCVD General Store (scvd.store)
