## The rules this proposal follows

1. **Nothing with a settle gets cut.** Money is the only vote that
   counts, and every item that took organic money stays.
2. **Nothing with an ATTEMPTED settle gets cut yet.** Four items
   (a_secret, luckies, standing_watch, recurring_patronage) had
   Bazaar settle calls that came back "processing" and never landed.
   Until the decline reasons say whether that was an empty wallet or
   a dropped rail, an attempted purchase is demand evidence, and
   cutting it would be answering a question we haven't read.
3. **A cut is a retirement, not a deletion.** Delisted from the menu;
   the id, its certificates, and its records resolve forever. The
   take table already buckets retired items under "Retired & other."
4. **n=1.** One real buyer this month. Cut duplicates, not
   categories.

## Gazette vs Almanac: fold it

The Gazette rack has sold zero. The Almanac's penny pages drew 19
organic 402s and real crawl interest — and the keeper writes the
Almanac anyway, because it's his register. Recommendation: the
Almanac becomes the store's one publication; the weekly Gazette
stops asking for keeper-time it doesn't earn back. The founding
edition stays printed (it's a record), the dispatch machinery stays
in the codebase dormant (approved-tips → dispatch may matter when
the Trading Post has traffic), and the "checkable story" job the
Gazette was for lives where it always actually lived: the ledger,
/stats, and dated Almanac pages. One voice, half the writing, same
verifiability.

## What this is not

Not a pivot. The store adds items every week it learns something;
this trims the duplicates the first data called out, so the next
addition starts from a shelf where every listing has a distinct job.
