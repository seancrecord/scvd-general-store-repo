# SCVD Partner Evidence Pilot Plan

Started September 24, 2026, at the keeper's request. Working plan, not a
published service promise. Accountable owner: Sean Record. Research and
evidence preparation: Codex in this task. Partner owners remain unassigned
until a counterpart actually agrees; naming a prospect is not an agreement.

## 1. Outcome

Establish one recurring external use of SCVD evidence: an outside team uses
an observation in a named engineering, documentation, release, listing, or
support decision and requests another bounded observation. Free repeat use
counts toward adoption; paid demand is measured separately. The early return
is better instruments, usable shared data, references, and relationships.

Do not measure success by defect count, signatures issued, speculative revenue,
or famous company names. A clean observation used in a decision can succeed.
A correct finding that nobody uses is research, not demonstrated adoption.

## 4. Phases and exit criteria

### Phase 1 — Establish what we can actually demonstrate

Target: September 24–30. Execution owner: Codex.

   bounds, hashes, and collector failures. Keep source statements distinct
   from SCVD observations, interpretations, and proposed next actions.
2. Verify the present state of Merit's selected issue, including comments,

   are leads, never current findings by themselves.
3. Check the candidate seller's published method, inputs, price, network,

4. Compare Browserbase's public purchase contract to our actual Launch Check

5. Prepare one short dossier per case and suggested outreach. Make each
   message useful without a sales pitch or a long onboarding demand.

Exit: three reviewable dossiers, source provenance, explicit unknowns,
one selected first approach, and no claim of independent reproduction unless
it was actually performed. A disproved or stale lead is a valid outcome.

### Phase 2 — Get one reciprocal working relationship

Target: October 1–14, conditional on a keeper-approved approach.
Accountable owner: Sean; preparation and evidence updates: Codex.

Start with one Merit evidence note. Approach the seller about the stated
request; keep its consent and readiness distinct from Merit's involvement.
Browserbase is next if a reviewer wants the study and the request-compatibility gap can be
handled within scope. No simultaneous three-company pilot program.

Ask for one technical reviewer, one product/workflow, a useful expected
outcome, and a short response on material observations. Accept asynchronous
issue replies as participation; do not demand a seven-label administrative
process before helping. Have the partner identify the next real decision
where evidence could be used.

Exit: named reviewer, agreed workflow and scope, publication/privacy terms,
and a specific decision use to test. An accepted free offer alone is not
adoption. After one reasonable follow-up and continued silence, hold the
prospect and try the next eligible one; silence is not a rejection finding.

### Phase 3 — Run one bounded buyer-path pilot

Target: a 30-day window starting only after Phase 2 completes.
Accountable owner: Sean; observation owner: Codex or the keeper as the
instrument requires; partner actions owned by the named reviewer.

- One product/workflow; begin with up to three representative endpoints only
  if that workflow requires them. This is a scope cap, not a coverage promise.
- One baseline using the appropriate existing free instruments and history.
- One authorized paid baseline only if the actual request is compatible and
  all existing spending controls hold. A recorded refusal or skipped attempt
  stays distinct from purchase success. Any repair purchase is separately
  scoped; never re-sign after an ambiguous outcome just to finish the study.
- Two cold runs initially, at most four if the variation answers a named
  question. Include one smaller-model/limited-context path where practical.
- Partner response on material findings. Distill reproducible mechanics into
  fixtures; preserve model-dependent confusion as qualitative evidence.
- One repair retest where a repair exists, followed by a decision-use and
  repeat-use review. No need to invent a repair when the baseline was clean.

Planning allowance: eight keeper hours per pilot, subject to measurement;
review scope at four hours. Stop expansion before promising extra work. Track
machine costs and research labor separately; these allowances are estimates,
not measurements. Initial research spend is zero. Future paid work uses the
published instrument cap at execution time, not a copied budget in this file.

Exit: evidence delivered and accounted for; partner response or explicit
nonresponse; named decision use (or none); requested repeat trigger (or none);
cost/time log; corrections and unresolved questions retained.

### Phase 4 — Repeat what worked, share what is reusable

Target: by December 22; depends on a completed useful pilot, not the date.
Accountable owner: Sean.

Run a second bounded cycle for the same team at a natural release/change.
Test whether the partner can retrieve and interpret the evidence without
handholding. Add a small export/renderer only after actual repeated assembly
shows the required fields. Any implementation goes on ROADMAP, not the desk.

Share sanitized fixtures, observation summaries, and correction/retest history
under the existing applicable terms. Use The Week's Doors and existing corpus
surfaces before creating another publication. Partner-specific raw material
remains private unless agreed otherwise. Do not imply endorsement or joint
certification from collaboration; independent registers remain independent.

Exit: recurring use demonstrated, or a dated decision to narrow/stop. Discuss
paid labor/convenience only after its scope and recurring cost are understood.
Free recurring use remains a separate, legitimate success outcome.

## 6. Dossier and finding contract

Every dossier includes a concrete partner question, exact sources, what we
observed, competing explanations, instrument compatibility, a falsifier,
the smallest useful follow-up, and current stage. Evidence hashes show file
integrity, not origin authentication or truth. Research captures are unsigned
unless a real signed SCVD artifact was obtained and verified separately.

For each finding retain:

- Identity: finding ID, URL/path, method, request/input digest, protocol,
  network, relevant client profile, observation time and instrument version.
- Observation: result, status, evidence references, bounds and missingness.
- Interpretation: hypothesis, alternatives and what would disprove it.
- Technical disposition: reported / reproduced / disputed / corrected /
  unresolved. Record a confirmed SCVD instrument error explicitly.
- Partner action: unknown / already known / investigating / changed /
  no action. This is independent of technical disposition.
- Usefulness: unknown / used in named decision / not useful. Keep the
  partner's actual response and date, not an inferred CRM label.
- Retest: not requested / awaiting change / passed / still reproduced /
  inconclusive / instrument changed; link original and successor evidence.
- Repeat: none / requested / agreed / completed, trigger and scope; separate
  free use, subsidized tests, organic activity, and paid demand.

Compare the same endpoint, method, inputs, terms and time context. A host-only
join is insufficient. A signature-valid artifact does not make its diagnosis
correct. An unsigned statement does not become signed when wrapped in a report.

## 7. Cold-agent method

Freeze task, model/version, host/tool access, entry point, context, budget and
stop boundary. The agent is new to the test context, not proven free of all
prior training knowledge. No coaching with the expected answer. Observe
discovery, price/cadence understanding, inputs, authorization preparation,
expected fulfillment, and recovery. Cold runs stop before payment unless
separately designated and authorized. Do not spawn or schedule runs merely
because this plan describes them; prepare their concrete task first.

Count every initiated run: completed, abandoned, interrupted, or instrument
failed. Show any conditional completion rate beside the full denominator.
Two runs are examples, not a prevalence estimate. Repetition alone does not
prove causation: a proposed fixture needs a narrow mechanism, an expected
behavior and a changed-field control. A deterministic check cannot fully
replace stochastic language comprehension testing.

## 8. Sharing and independence

Existing code/data/content licenses remain unchanged. Free pilots have dates,
limits and specified outputs; they are not free-forever commitments. Share
public protocol observations and sanitized fixtures where permitted. Do not
publish paid payloads, access handles, personal data, or confidential partner
material merely because acquisition began at a public URL. Agree retention
for new nonpublic material before collecting it; do not create a new blanket
deletion or permanence promise here.

Peer findings go private-first under HOUSE_RULES 51. Keep each instrument's
register and interpretation separate; no co-signature implying joint
certification. Publish qualifying unresolved disagreements with sources,
derivations, and the notice date. A partner can correct us without paying;
payment never buys suppression or a different standard.

## 9. Measures and stop rules

| Measure | Denominator / evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Decision use | Completed pilots with a named decision influenced / all completed pilots; partner response required |
| Repeat use | Completed pilots with a requested repeat, and separately an actually completed repeat / all completed pilots |
| Useful findings | Findings used in a named decision / findings reviewed; unreviewed findings separately counted |
| Reproduction | Reproduced / attempted; distinguish partner reproduction from our local fixture |
| Repair confirmation | Retests passing / retests attempted; claimed fixes and untested fixes separately counted |
| Instrument errors | Confirmed errors / findings assessed; describe severity and correction rather than hiding behind a percentage |
| Agent outcomes | Completed / all initiated runs, with abandoned/interrupted/instrument-failed counts |
| Cost | Keeper time, research time, actual compute/storage/payment cost per pilot; unknown remains unknown |
| Distribution | Actual partner links, integrations, citations or downstream use; customer size alone does not count |

One useful free repeat is the first adoption milestone, not a market-size
claim. A severe measurement error stops the affected claim immediately. Fix
and recheck the instrument before expanding. Do not wait for an arbitrary
20% error threshold. Hold a pilot with no reviewer or no response after the
agreed review window; do not interpret silence as technical agreement. Stop
bespoke development if nobody can name the decision it would improve.
