## §1 — Tiers on the passport (roadmap N7)

```
Doctrine change, then a feature.

DOCTRINE. Replace "never a score, a rating or a ranking" everywhere it appears (storefront, /what, /attestation, listing description, llms.txt, skill.md, the x402-list row) with: "never a ranking, and never a verdict without its derivation and denominator beside it." Rankings stay forbidden — nothing on this store ever orders one host against another. Derived verdicts with published rules are now in scope. Add a dated note to /criteria explaining the change and why. If any signed row or paid artifact currently cites the old sentence, it keeps its bytes; do not resign.

FEATURE. A named tier on every endpoint passport, derived at read from the signed per-host history in the corpus. Default rule (put it on /criteria verbatim, and it is the only place it is typed):

- observed:    at least 1 signed round
- established: ready in at least 4 of the last 4 weekly rounds, no unreachable
- standing:    ready in at least 8 of the last 8 weekly rounds, no unreachable
- broken:      most recent signed observation (round or paid refresh) is not_ready or unreachable
- indeterminate: fewer rounds than the rule needs, or our coverage of this host was suspect in the window

The tier is a function of (rounds in window, ready count, latest observation, coverage_suspect). Every rendering of the tier prints the fraction it came from (e.g. "established — 4 of 4, W33–W36") and links the rows. No ratio without its denominator, no tier without its rows. Newest-wins fold is the same one the passport and profile already share; a paid refresh that finds the door broken moves the tier to broken that hour.

RENDER on: passport page, passport JSON, the freshness chip (chip carries tier text, dark for broken/indeterminate, SELF stays on our own), hosted profile, /corpus/host/{host}.json, and a new /corpus/tiers.json that lists every host's tier with its fraction — unordered, alphabetical by host, because ordered by tier is a ranking.

TESTS, written red first:
- tier derives from fixture rows; typing a tier anywhere fails the build (extend derived-not-typed)
- a host at 3 of 4 is observed not established; at 4 of 4 with one unreachable is not established
- coverage_suspect true in window forces indeterminate regardless of readiness
- a paid refresh finding broken flips passport, chip, profile and tiers.json in the same hour
- tiers.json is alphabetical and contains no rank or position field
- old doctrine sentence absent from every public surface; new sentence present on the same list
- /criteria carries the rule and a dated note

Do not change the battery. Do not add a numeric 0–100 anything. When done, tell me what existing copy contradicted the new doctrine and whether any of it warrants a corrections entry.
```

Notes on §1, from the agent that filed it (not a ruling):

- The doctrine sentence is inked copy on every surface it names,
  and the x402-list row is a press. The doctrine commit is its own
  branch ahead of the feature, so the sentence swap can be read on
  its own diff.
- "Do not resign" reaches the corpus snapshot rows and the paid
  artifacts that quote the old sentence. Rows keep their bytes; the
  new sentence applies from the next signed round.
- The word "established" will be read as approval by a merchant
  who pastes the chip. The fraction beside it is the whole defence,
  which is why the prompt makes it non-optional on every rendering.

## §2 — The Case File (roadmap N8)

```
New paid item on the observation shelf: the_case_file. One URL, one signed artifact, holding everything this store already observed about a single agent purchase, assembled for the human who has to decide what went wrong. Neither party controls it.

INPUTS: tx_hash (required; shape picks the chain). Optional: mandate_id, endpoint url, payer, recipient, expected_amount_usdc, and the buyer's own claim as free text up to 1000 chars (stored verbatim, marked declared, never checked).

WHAT IT BINDS, each as its own section with present/absent stated explicitly:
1. settlement — a fresh settlement_attestation on the tx (status, block, confirmations, amounts, counterparties)
2. reconciliation — if EVM: settlement_reconciliation (cap vs moved, cap_source, verdict as that item already defines it)
3. mandate — if mandate_id given: the stored mandate record, declared cap vs settled amount stated side by side, never enforced
4. door — everything the corpus and any watches hold on the endpoint host over the 7 days around the tx: rounds, verdicts, watch rows if a watch was running, passport tier at the time if derivable. If we never observed the host: not_observed, which is an answer about our books.
5. delivery — if the buyer supplies a launch_check id or our fulfillment log has an entry for this tx: what it says. Otherwise: "delivery not observed by this store" in full weight, because this is the section a dispute usually turns on and we usually do not have it.
6. the buyer's declared claim, verbatim, labeled declared
7. gaps — a list of every section that came back absent, with the reason, counted against us

NO VERDICT. The case file says what was observed and what was not. It never says who was wronged. That sentence goes on the artifact.

CONFLICT. If recipient is one of our own declared wallets or the endpoint host is scvd.store, the artifact prints "this store is a party to this purchase" on its face and links /rights and /fulfillment-log. Do not refuse — a buyer disputing us should get the same instrument — but name it.

PRICE: $0.25. Sum of the underlying attestations is under a dime; the rest is the assembly. Idempotent by tx_hash + mandate_id within 24h.

OUTPUTS: signed JSON, evidence hash bound into the certificate's attests, stable /case/{id} URL free forever, verify at /api/verify. Menu, OpenAPI, llms.txt, skill.md, x402 discovery doc, /samples, and the ClawHub bundle all updated in the same commit — the price test from 2026-08-31 must see it.

TESTS, red first: every section present on a full fixture; every section absent on an empty one with gaps enumerated; conflict line appears iff we are a party; no field in the artifact contains the words fault, wronged, liable, or a verdict enum; declared fields never influence any observed field; price in every copy map matches MENU_ITEMS.

Before writing code, list which existing items you are composing and whether any of them needs a change to be composable (I expect the watch lookup by host+window does not exist yet).
```

Notes on §2:

- Section 4 cites the passport tier "if derivable", so N8 follows
  N7 in the order; nothing else in it waits on the tier.
- The composability list the prompt asks for is the first commit
  of the branch, before any code: settlement_attestation,
  settlement_reconciliation, the mandate record, corpus host rows,
  watch rows by host and window (expected missing), the fulfillment
  log by tx (expected missing), launch_check by id.
- Rule 23b holds: this is an instrument for the person deciding,
  not a court. The no-verdict sentence on the artifact is what
  keeps it on this side of the line.
