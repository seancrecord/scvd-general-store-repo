### The token, and nothing else

At https://huggingface.co/settings/tokens → **Create new token** →
**Fine-grained**:

- Name: `scvd-x402-verifier-push`
- Under *Repositories*: tick **Write access to contents/settings of
  repos you select** — nothing else; leave every user/org/inference
  permission unticked. (The Space does not exist until step 3 below,
  so on the first pass select nothing; after `hf repo create`, edit
  the token and select the Space by name.)
- Expiry: set one. A week is plenty.

Never the classic account-wide "write" token. Never any value from
the store's repository, Cloudflare, or a wallet: the Space needs no
secret, and a Space holding one is a Space with something to leak.
