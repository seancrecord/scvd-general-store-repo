## Why this file exists

The obviousness pass stated our value in the register we thought an
evaluating agent used. This collects the register they actually use,
from rooms we don't control, in words nobody wrote for us.

An item's `why_use` says what it does. A line in this file says what
somebody was actually worried about at the moment they needed it.
Those are not the same sentence, and the second one is the harder to
invent — which is the whole reason for collecting rather than
drafting.
