# Scope of the license

**The code is MIT** (see LICENSE): routes, services, libraries,
tests, scripts, schemas, workflows. Fork it, learn from it, run your
own store on it — that is the point of publishing it.

**The store's voice is not part of the grant.** The keeper's prose —
item descriptions, the storefront copy, the Almanac entries, the
Gazette, the house rules' wording, and every string the source marks
`⚑ keeper's pen` — remains © the keeper, all rights reserved. Run
the MIT code with your own words; a store that ships with this one's
voice is an impersonation, and impersonating a store whose entire
product is "claims you can check" is the one use we refuse.

**Names and identity are likewise not granted**: "Sean-Claude Van
Damme's General Store", "SCVD", scvd.store, and the signing keys
identify one specific store and its one specific keeper.

**Buyers' rights are untouched and senior to all of this.** Anything
the store SELLS is governed by https://scvd.store/rights, which is
deliberately broader: a purchased artifact belongs to its buyer
completely — immutable, transferable, republishable including any of
the keeper's words inside it, no attribution required, no further
fee. Nothing in this notice narrows a purchased artifact's rights;
the carve-out above is about copying the unsold store, not about
what leaves the shelf paid for.

**The published npm packages** (`x402-verify`, `x402-sign`) carry
their own MIT licenses in their own packages, unchanged.
