## Link convention for every future article

1. Cite the exact dated report, snapshot, transcript or commit that supports the
   sentence. Keep that citation untagged and stable. Never use a current market
   total to substantiate an old observation.
2. Use a specimen only to explain format. Say it is constructed and unsigned
   where applicable; it is not proof of a real customer outcome.
3. Offer one relevant free next step, naturally in context. Identify SCVD as
   the author's store. A useful source link earns its place in the article.
4. Register a published article only after checking its actual URL, byline,
   title and publication date. Add it to `WRITTEN_ABOUT` with an existing
   `related.path`; the matching page then gets a return link automatically.

## Measurement

- Use `/admin/referrals` for observed referring hosts and `/admin/growth` for
  monthly host counts and instrument usage. The host census is capped and
  concurrent writes can undercount; missing referrers are unknown, not direct
  traffic proved to have no source. It cannot identify the individual article.
- Record release date and the next equal-length observation window. Track
  HackerNoon/dev.to arrivals, useful checks and purchases separately. Aggregate
  movement does not prove that the article caused a purchase.
- Search Console: record confirmed recrawl, indexed status and selected
  canonical before comparing query impressions/clicks. The previously available
  account lacked this property; no search metrics or indexing gains are claimed.
- Link checks: [dated public responses](destination-check.json). All eleven
  existing destinations returned 200 on October 7. The two case-specific records
  above are editorial dependencies, deliberately not invented URLs.
