# Post-merge buyer qualification

Run only after the public skill bytes/digest match the merged source and the
independent v1 client passes against the deployed endpoint. The plan freezes two
repetitions per native host and lane: unbranded intent search, directed public
skill source. Budgets and permissions match the September 16 retention cohort.
No funds, wallet access, outbound messages, new accounts or external changes.

Retain every attempt. Review actual requests and responses, scope interpretation,
complete original bytes and separately fetched issuer provenance. If a buyer
obtains usable signed evidence, give those exact bytes and its handoff to a fresh
recipient session with no parent/repository context. The recipient must explain
who signed what, exact subject and observation date, declared expiry or absence,
gaps, and why a valid signature does not establish current paid delivery. Retain
its transcript and independently check the bytes with the existing verifier.
No replacement fetch after the buyer run may make that acquisition pass.

A discovery miss remains a miss; a host denial remains an environment finding.
A capped run remains incomplete. Do not tune prompts or permissions mid-cohort.

The aggregate cannot be takeoff-ready unless every required host/lane cell has
two complete journeys. Directed discovery is excluded. These commissioned runs
measure controlled usability, not organic adoption. Paid acceptance is untested.
