**Resource Name:** SCVD General Store — ERC-8004 discovery implementation

**URL:** https://github.com/seancrecord/scvd-general-store-repo

**Category:** Builder Projects / Community Projects

**Description:** An open-source evidence observatory for agentic commerce, with an ERC-8004 identity on Base and a public registration document connecting its MCP, A2A, OASF and x402 discovery surfaces.

**Why it should be included:** Builders can inspect a deployed registration and the code that produces it, including endpoint-domain acknowledgment and cross-protocol service metadata. The canonical document and source are freely accessible. This is a community implementation, not a protocol certification or an official reference implementation.

- OASF record: https://scvd.store/agents/general-store
- A2A Agent Card: https://scvd.store/.well-known/agent-card.json
- Developer documentation: https://scvd.store/developers
