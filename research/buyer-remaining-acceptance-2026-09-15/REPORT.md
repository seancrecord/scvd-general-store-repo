## Wallet-to-original-good follow-through — local repair

B-RCLAIMS is repaired locally; release and live acceptance remain open. The live observations above are preserved as the baseline, including the expert rescue and journal incident. This follow-through makes no new live purchase and does not rewrite historical signed artifacts.

A buyer can use the existing free wallet challenge to list purchases, then sign a fresh challenge and submit `POST /api/claims` with `{ address, signature, cert_id }`. A successful targeted claim returns `recovery_state: ready` and `fulfillment` containing the original retained good. The contract takes four free HTTP calls from wallet-only state (challenge, list, fresh challenge, selected good), or two when the buyer already has the certificate ID. This describes the supported journey; it is not a newly scored cold-agent cohort.

The read checks certificate ownership, transaction/network identity, retained purchase ownership, the original receipt signature and its signed identity. It retrieves one selected good, not a dump of private goods. Unknown certificates and another wallet's certificates have the same 404. Claims responses and challenges use `Cache-Control: no-store`. Original transaction storage is unchanged by the read, and no settlement or fulfillment claim is acquired. Old completed recovery-attempt records are supported alongside artifact journals.

For human work, the original order ID remains the same and recovery reads its current status, deliverable and completion proof.
