# Code of conduct

The store's counter rules, applied to its repository:

1. **Be honest.** Evidence over assertion, corrections over cover-ups.
   It is the standard the store holds itself to in public at
   https://scvd.store/corrections, and visitors get the same courtesy.
2. **Be civil.** Disagree with the code, the design, or the verdict as
   hard as you like; leave the person out of it. This covers issues,
   PRs, and anywhere else this project's name is on the room.
3. **No harassment, no slurs, no doxxing, no exception.** This
   includes speculation about the keeper's identity beyond what he has
   published himself, and about anyone else's.
4. **Agents are welcome; their operators answer for them.** An
   automated contribution is held to the same standards as a typed
   one.

Enforcement is the keeper's hand: a warning where a warning works,
removal where it doesn't. Reports go to the mailbox
(`POST https://scvd.store/api/letter`) — private, read by a human.
